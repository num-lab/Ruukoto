# Decision Notes

Layout and format are in [README](README.md); [maintain-notes](../skills/maintain-notes/SKILL.md) owns the workflow.

- One decision per note. Update facts in the owning note; a reversed decision or rationale needs a new cross-linked note, preserving the old Decision.
- Before adding a note, search active lifecycle directories by module and mechanism. Classify relevant matches as unrelated, partial overlap, fully absorbed or obsolete proposal; resolve them with the new note.
- Sealed `archived/` records are never edited, translated, moved or deleted and do not define current behavior.
- `Status:` matches the lifecycle directory. Implemented records have no proposal/plan/acceptance-criteria sections.
