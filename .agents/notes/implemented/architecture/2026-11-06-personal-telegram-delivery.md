# Agent Note: Telegram cards and complete deliveries retain original authority and receipts

Status: implemented

## Problem

Personal already retains original decisions, questions, reports and final files, but its Telegram replies bound text independently and its owner controls depended on queried menus. A proactive card needs verifiable displayed-message identity, and a complete result needs ordered send facts for every piece. Repeating work or replacing the result after a lost response would duplicate effects or rewrite the original uncertainty. [Issue #44](https://github.com/num-lab/Ruukoto/issues/44) applies [the approved Personal scope](https://github.com/num-lab/Ruukoto/issues/34) to Telegram after the desktop milestone, without adding another business workflow.

## Decision

Telegram projects each original business decision into its own durable display. Operation families still own approval, question answers, effect statements, stopping and allowed actions. The display fixes original platform, bot, owner, generation, admission, occurrence, selection, body and delivery reference. Opaque random callback tokens bind that display and one explicit action; the token index and immutable display admission commit together. Current source and canonical action eligibility are checked again before accepting a choice. A repeated or redisplayed card shares the original decision, rather than granting another approval.

Callbacks require the bound owner in an accessible bot-authored message in that owner's private chat. The persisted token, original message ID and current generation must agree. Inline, forwarded, inaccessible, stale or contradictory contexts fail closed. Direct replies likewise select a confirmed original card message. A long card attaches its keyboard only to the final text piece after all earlier text sends are confirmed, so a button cannot approve an incomplete preceding body.

For a direct reply to a one-shot approval card, complete approval words after trimming outer whitespace are exactly `好`, `好的`, `可以`, `行`, `同意`, `确认`, `ok` and `OK`; rejection words are exactly `不`, `不要`, `不行`, `先别`, `拒绝`, `算了` and `取消`. The immutable original display must actually offer that one-shot approval or refusal action; a tool or MCP selection alone does not establish an approval card. Listed numbered choices remain valid. The host does not strip punctuation, add synonyms or interpret `嗯` or `是` as approval. Nonmatches and non-direct replies remain ordinary chat. A card reply of `取消` rejects that operation; ordinary cancellation addresses the identified assignment. Question answers, human effect statements and existing fixed-service standing permission retain their own explicit actions.

Telegram question choices use the original question and draft records. A single choice submits directly, multi-select waits for Done, and a batch of questions ends with review. Free answers belong to the selected question. Child answers return once through the asking ToolTask under its original work and permissions; they do not create a separate direct reply execution. The eventual report uses that work's validated publication.

Callback acknowledgement and terminal card editing are separate durable transport tasks with admissions, claims and observations. Business commit does not wait for the platform acknowledgement or edit. Failed, interrupted or unknown transport outcomes never undo a committed choice or revive a terminal action. An explicit redisplay creates a new display of the same canonical record and retains the previous uncertain send. Backend validation keeps old messages safe even when editing fails.

The card scanner starts after configuration recovery. Its scan resumes the shared Harness, even when Telegram has no enabled entry, so starting it earlier can run a saved child while model settings are suspended and fail otherwise resumable work. IM configuration publication restarts its receiver without enabling the scheduler; online instances are already running, while startup owns the first resume after recovery. The initial scan reads canonical pending records after recovery; it does not depend on observing the startup commits through its subscription.

A terminal failed, faulted or orphaned acknowledgement keeps its task outcome. Recovery validates the original admission, claim and indexed history before appending one canceled observation if unclaimed or one unknown observation if claimed; it never repeats the acknowledgement. Terminal card edits follow the same rule. The shared delivery verifier likewise completes all integrity reads before appending recovery observations, in a separate early commit before caller reconciliation. Scheduled delivery mirrors append their own recovery observation only after their history and orphan scans finish.

Human applied/not-applied choices append immutable claims with the original source, time and displayed card, while preserving the original operation result. A claim establishes neither stopping nor delivery. Telegram Cua choices enter the existing canonical recovery action task with the original IM actor, operation, occurrence and captured pause/observation binding. Exact cleanup, fresh observation and acceptance keep their existing order; stopping the whole recorded Linux sandbox requires its separate explicit choice because unsaved state may be lost. No current default permission or arbitrary chat answer substitutes for that recovery authority.

`查看原电脑现场` is an explicit host-only read intent for the exact saved recovery observation. It fixes the original request, private entry, displayed occurrence, pause, execution and image references, then uses the shared delivery ledger to send complete original screenshot documents. The loader and every send claim recheck the committed owner request and current scene binding; a new observation or entry generation stops remaining old images. This inspection neither creates a final-output publication nor enters `/结果`. Acceptance becomes available only after every image in that observation has confirmed delivery; historical acceptance verifies those confirmations before the original action admission, so a later view cannot retroactively justify an earlier choice. Telegram cannot establish that the client opened an image; the card asks the owner to open and inspect it before accepting. Unknown image sends retain their facts and require another explicit view request. The whole-sandbox stop card warns about unsaved state before the choice. Legacy native resume also refuses an IM-bound pause.

### Complete original delivery

`personal-im-delivery.ts` owns versioned transport intent and observations for owner replies, question continuations, reports, scheduled results and card text. The optional parent task records lifecycle ownership; it grants no model-work authority. One named reference binds a stable key to one task. Registration persists exact text, original source, original reply message and ordered parts with the admission and index in one Session transaction. Same-key recovery requires identical input and parent; it cannot silently replace an unknown delivery.

Text pieces contain at most 4096 UTF-16 code units without splitting a valid surrogate pair. Concatenation reproduces the exact original string, including whitespace and combining characters; delivery adds no continuation headings. Each send claim fixes content, sequence, attempt, source and selected configuration version before dispatch. Claim and checkpoint commit together. Source checks and pinned sender selection use the [existing short platform mutex](2026-10-25-personal-im-updates.md); network calls and model waits remain outside it.

Every new claim checks original entry activity, cancellation, parent lifecycle and the caller's canonical authorization. Reports recheck contributing sources, instructions and covered work before each piece. Temporary uncertainty about an accepted owner input waits for committed changes outside the mutex. Changed requirements cancel remaining old pieces while retaining the original publication and any started send's outcome. Questions and schedules verify their original answer receipt or plan/result and source limits.

Earlier text must be confirmed before the next piece starts. A known text failure or any uncertain send stops every later text/file part. Only a verified Telegram `not_sent` rate-limit rejection can defer the same part, within three total attempts derived from persisted claims. Invalid or overflowing retry deadlines finish as a known failure. Known file failures remain independent and may allow a later file to proceed; an uncertain file send stops the remainder. Text and file results are separate, so pending or failed media cannot hide text already confirmed.

Restart at a claimed, unobserved send records `effect_unknown` and stops pending later parts. Cancel stops pending pieces; abort or interruption preserves uncertainty for started pieces. Caller recovery waits for the exact delivery task and reconciles its canonical text state and last confirmed text message ID. It does not infer uncertainty solely from a caller's old `sending` mirror. Immutable report failure entries retain their historical delivery state, reconstructed from claims and observations before that entry; a later confirmed send changes the current mirror without overwriting failure history.

Published output parts identify the committed publication and selected output. The owning reader checks membership, original source and artifact identity; the ledger validates complete bytes, the 8 MiB limit and SHA-256 before document upload. Published output parts send only owner-requested final files/screenshots, using original stored bytes. Partial or oversized capture stays visibly undeliverable. Existing eight-file publication limits remain. Failed transfer never reruns a successful write or model work, and publication, business success and delivery remain distinct.

`/结果` lists at most eight original results per page for the same private entry. A confirmed selection can request all saved text or a specified stored piece. Explicit retrieval records the original delivery reference and ascending text-part indices as a new requested intent, requires the same platform/bot/owner/generation, and retains the exact selected original text. It adds no keyboard or file parts, leaves the earlier receipt unchanged and never reexecutes work. Normal multipart delivery has no Continue gate.

Production delivery dispatch and reopen verify reciprocal ownership: each parent-backed delivery belongs to the exact reply, report, question continuation or scheduled result; parentless intents belong to an issued card or an accepted explicit text/image request. A source identity alone cannot authorize a fabricated send. Immutable Telegram/QQ received entries and accepted callbacks point back to their indexed owner receipt, and every accepted receipt points to its original entry. Truly historical records without such entries keep the old branch; deleting a new acceptance field cannot turn it into legacy data. Inbound media retains its separate receive provenance.

New Telegram caller records mark complete formatting and retain their canonical delivery reference. Historical reply fields keep their original formatter validation. Old pending formatted replies can register the original complete assistant or scheduled result once; old sending/unknown records are not replayed. The existing active-version-1-report cutover rule remains. Bidirectional verification checks index/task/admission identity, exact originals, parent and retrieval backlinks, ordered transitions, checkpoint/outcome agreement and orphan records. A claimed send cannot become simply canceled, and QQ cannot acquire Telegram's rate-limit retry rule.

Schedule scope comparison excludes exactly the host-only `personal-im-delivery`, `personal-telegram-displays` and `personal-telegram-callbacks` extensions from detached current/original comparisons. These extensions provide transport tasks without model tools or execution authority; adding them during an upgrade must not cancel a proven unsent historical result. The stored creation scope and immutable admission remain intact. All business extension/tool differences, material/configuration snapshots, entry generation and the existing publication ceiling still apply, including active scheduled sources and every send claim. This narrows [the original complete-extension comparison](2026-10-18-personal-durable-scheduled-work.md) only for those named transport extensions.

### Presence and presentation

Typing is best effort while ordinary owner reply work is pending. It adds no canned acknowledgement, model-speed promise or persisted acceptance fact; failure cannot change work or delivery. Normal host copy identifies the decision, result or next owner action in plain language. Explicitly requested paths, quotations, logs and technical evidence remain intact. Generated model expression requires scenario evidence beyond deterministic host-string tests.

## Alternatives considered

- Require unchanged transport extensions for old schedules: reuses exact comparison, but cancels genuine pending results after adding host send tasks despite unchanged execution authority. Ignore only the three known transport extensions in comparison copies.
- Extend each caller's send state independently: retains local ownership, but duplicates ordering and lets unknown-send recovery drift. Share transport facts while callers keep business authority.
- Approve from a callback token alone: a compact interface, but cannot establish original message, private owner or current action eligibility. Bind token, confirmed display and canonical action together.
- Retry an entire result under a fresh key after uncertainty: can restore readable output, but duplicates already received pieces. Preserve the receipt and require explicit original-text retrieval.
- Retry every verified rate limit indefinitely: each refusal proves non-send, but a finite result can remain live and append unbounded claims. Bound each part with its durable attempt count.
- Wait for all files before reporting text delivery: presents one result, but hides confirmed text during media waits or failures. Expose text and file outcomes separately.
- Replace a failure's delivery field with the latest state: easier to query, but rewrites history when a held send later confirms. Verify the immutable snapshot at its entry boundary.
- Rely on card editing to disable actions: avoids backend rechecks, but platform failures can leave stale buttons visible. Canonical eligibility rejects them independently of edits.
- Start card scanning immediately after display verification: registers projections earlier, but its shared runtime resume can race unfinished configuration recovery. Start after settings recovery and derive pending cards from the initial scan.
- Treat recovery screenshots as final publications: reuses file-result metadata, but falsely turns an inspection into a completed business result. An explicit recovery read shares only original-byte delivery and retains its original scene authority.

## Consequences

Telegram retains complete original results and actionable cards while main continues handling independent input. Costs are immutable display/token/acknowledgement facts and one claim/observation chain per delivery part. Conservative uncertainty stops the remainder and can require owner inspection or explicit retrieval. Entry replacement invalidates pending actions and sends without redirecting them; platform calls and durable commits remain separate systems.

Offline checks exercise exact Unicode, original source and formatter recovery, bounded retries, callback/edit separation, immutable claims, parent-fault reconciliation, partial output and actual process crashes. They do not establish real Telegram rendering, account identity, Linux execution or model behavior. Integrated real-account, isolated Linux and configured-model acceptance remain tracked in [Issue #44](https://github.com/num-lab/Ruukoto/issues/44); this implemented decision does not declare that milestone accepted or close related acceptance Issues.
