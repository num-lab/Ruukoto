# Semantic Self-Check

Validators check structure; the author and independent reviewer check meaning. Use this internally rather than reciting it in chat.

- Is the problem a genuine choice, or a fact the repository can answer? Research facts before asking the owner.
- Does the motivation stand without the chosen solution? Is the trigger and consequence of inaction clear?
- Can a contributor act on the decision, and does the rationale explain why it beats the real alternatives?
- Are alternatives genuinely considered, fairly stated and rejected for concrete constraints? Do not invent a minimum count.
- Do consequences include both costs and benefits? Does a simplification name its limit and revisit signal?
- Are verification claims tied to an observable path, actual command/environment or measured baseline? Missing evidence remains explicit.
- Can a new reader resolve provenance and distinguish current facts, historical observations and proposals?

Fix identified gaps within confirmed scope. Report only unresolved gaps or decisions requiring the owner, with a concrete proposed remedy. Semantic review is not a second syntax gate and owner approval does not turn missing required evidence into a pass.
